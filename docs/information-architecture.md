# Information architecture — working proposal

## Languages and URLs

Spanish is proposed as the default language and English as the explicit secondary route.

| Content | Spanish | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | `/en` |
| Exhibitions & Projects | `/exposiciones-y-proyectos` | `/en/exhibitions-and-projects` |
| Project detail | `/exposiciones-y-proyectos/[slug]` | `/en/exhibitions-and-projects/[slug]` |
| Bio | `/bio` | `/en/bio` |
| Selected Artworks | `/obras-seleccionadas` | `/en/selected-artworks` |
| Artwork detail | `/obras-seleccionadas/[slug]` | `/en/selected-artworks/[slug]` |
| Procesos situados | `/procesos-situados` | `/en/situated-processes` |
| Process entry | `/procesos-situados/[slug]` | `/en/situated-processes/[slug]` |
| Prácticas compartidas | `/practicas-compartidas` | `/en/shared-practices` |
| Shared practice entry | `/practicas-compartidas/[slug]` | `/en/shared-practices/[slug]` |

Every translated page should declare canonical and reciprocal `hreflang` URLs. A language switch should preserve the current content when a translation exists.

## Section roles

### Exhibitions & Projects

The principal narrative archive. It combines exhibitions, installations and long-form projects, with filters for year, territory, material and type.

### Bio

Artist statement, short biography, portrait, selected CV and downloadable full CV. It should foreground place, material research and institutional context.

### Selected Artworks

A deliberately edited group of individual works, not a catalogue of everything produced. Entries may link back to the exhibition, project or studio investigation in which they originated.

### Procesos situados

A process and research journal: material research, workshop processes, tests, formulations, wild-clay collection, urban landscape and personal archive. Entries may be ongoing and can relate to projects and artworks.

### Prácticas compartidas

Collective research, workshops, encounters and co-creation. This section documents the relational and collaborative dimension of the practice without mixing it into the individual studio archive.

## Shared relationships

Projects, artworks and studio entries remain separate editorial forms but share taxonomies and bidirectional relationships. A project can reference artworks and studio research; an artwork can point back to its project; a studio entry can document the material process behind either.

## Homepage sequence

1. Full-bleed featured image with artist name and concise practice statement.
2. Two or three featured exhibitions/projects.
3. Short bio introduction.
4. Selected artworks in a quieter, object-focused rhythm.
5. Studio journal feature with field/process imagery.
6. Contact, representation and social links.

## Visual principle

The archive and metadata use a precise editorial system. Home and detail pages use spacious, image-led compositions. Motion is limited to navigation state, image reveal and restrained transitions that respect reduced-motion preferences.
